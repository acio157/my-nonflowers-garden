# Miró — style reference

The fact base every Miró constraint is built against. Anchored to his mature Surrealist "dream" period, roughly 1923 to 1945 (The Hunter through the Constellations). His early work (The Farm, 1921 to 22) is dense and near-representational; his late work goes even emptier with heavier black. We build to the 1920s to 40s.

Each point is FACT (traceable to a museum source read directly) or INTERPRETIVE (critical or symbolic reading that varies by author).

## Palette
- FACT: color is flat, unmodulated, non-naturalistic. Solid zones, no shading or modeling.
- FACT: bright saturated primaries (red, blue, yellow) are the loud accents against neutral or colored grounds.
- FACT: black is a full structural color, not just an outline, and grows heavier over his career.
- FACT: grounds are often not white. Often a flat colored or warm neutral field (ochre, tan, saturated blue in late work).
- CONVENTION (not a verbatim museum quote): the shorthand palette "red, blue, yellow, green, black, white." Green is the least emphasized.

## Line
- FACT: thin, calligraphic, drawn rather than painted.
- FACT: weight varies deliberately. Fine wiry line plus an occasional heavy curved contour, both black.
- FACT: continuous and connective. Links and encloses floating shapes into a web.
- INTERPRETIVE: often called childlike, spontaneous, automatic.

## Form vocabulary
- FACT: biomorphic shapes. Rounded, curving, organic, hovering between figure and abstraction.
- FACT: recurring signs, museum-documented: stars, birds, crescent moon, women / "personages," dots and spots, the ladder. (The eye is well established but softer-sourced.)
- FACT: in the Constellations the signs recombine into all-over networks rather than staying separate objects.
- INTERPRETIVE: the signs are often called his "personal alphabet." Specific meanings vary by author.

## Composition
- FACT: a flat color ground is laid first, signs placed on top.
- FACT: forms float weightlessly. Little or no perspective, horizon, or ground plane.
- FACT: "constellated," all-over distribution. Signs scattered across the whole field, often tied by line.
- FACT: generous negative space. Not filled edge to edge. Emptiness increases over time.

## Technique and surface
- FACT: flat, matte, even color in unshaded zones. No impasto modeling as the norm.
- FACT: black works two ways at once, as contour beside or around shapes and as independent drawn marks, laid on top last.
- FACT / NUANCE: automatism is documented but partly staged. The honest model is a chance-feeling ground plus a deliberately drawn layer of signs on top. Pure randomness misrepresents him.
